# DraftGap Ultra

This repo enhances the original DraftGap with extra controls and analysis features. This was made for personal use with no guarantee of accuracy.

Some of the new features added to this fork:

- Win-rate deltas make champion suggestions easier to scan.
- Configurable champion win-rate influence lets you explore off-meta picks while still considering overall win rate.
- Configurable role matchup and duo influence lets you choose which roles to counter or synergize with.
- Configurable situational adjustment accounts for the drafts champions are usually picked in while using direct interactions for revealed picks.
- Configurable blindability scoring rewards reliable matchups and synergies when picks are still unknown.
- Configurable team composition scoring rewards picks that fill your team's needs and respond to revealed enemies.
- Flex-pick uncertainty analysis considers every valid role assignment, weighted by its likelihood.
- Desktop-only build analysis recommends up to three core builds and rune options from Lolalytics statistics for your selected champion.
- Small usability improvements remember your hover setting and window size and position.

---

DraftGap.com is a site and desktop application which helps you pick and draft your League of Legends team. It suggests champions based on the meta, their matchups with every opponent champion, every ally duo, and configurable composition fundamentals. Matchup, duo, damage, and scaling inputs come from statistics; frontline, engage, peel, crowd-control, waveclear, and sustained-damage capabilities use a small, reviewed champion profile.

If you ever wondered what champion to pick, which is the best champion you could have picked, or if the game was truly lost in draft, DraftGap is for you.

Find it at [draftgap.com](https://draftgap.com), or download the app, which integrates with the League client to automatically synchronize with the current draft in champ select.
